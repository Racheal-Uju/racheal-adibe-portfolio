# 📖 About Me

I am an experienced Igbo language tutor with over four years of teaching experience and more than **1,500 teaching hours**, helping learners from diverse backgrounds build confidence in speaking, reading and understanding the Igbo language. I hold a **Bachelor of Arts in Linguistics** and have worked with adults, children and heritage learners, tailoring each lesson to meet their individual goals and learning styles.

My teaching philosophy is centred on creating an encouraging and interactive learning environment where students feel confident making progress at their own pace. I combine practical language instruction with cultural context, ensuring learners not only understand the language but also appreciate the traditions and heritage behind it.
