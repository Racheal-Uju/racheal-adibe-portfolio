About

A linguistics background, a learner-first classroom.

I'm an Igbo language tutor with over four years of teaching experience and more than 1,500 teaching hours, helping learners from diverse backgrounds build real confidence in speaking, reading, and understanding Igbo. I hold a Bachelor's degree in Linguistics and have taught adults, children, and heritage learners through engaging, learner-centred lessons tailored to their individual goals.

My approach combines structured language instruction with cultural immersion, so lessons stay practical as well as enjoyable. I lean on conversation practice, storytelling, flashcards, sentence-building, and pronunciation drills to keep the classroom supportive and paced to each student.

Whether you're learning Igbo for travel, family connections, cultural heritage, or personal growth, my goal is the same: make the language accessible, engaging, and rewarding — and help you use it confidently in everyday situations.
